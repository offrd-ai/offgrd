FAQ

Questions coaches ask.

Does it replace Hudl?

No — it rides on it. Keep your film in Hudl; export the breakdown and upload it to OFFGRD as-is (.xlsx or .csv, no conversion). Hudl stores what happened. OFFGRD tells you what's coming and teaches your roster what to do about it.

Does it really work without internet?

Yes — and we test it in airplane mode on real hardware. Predictions, both Callers, the live analysis panel, the playbook, and training all run on-device; the app even cold-boots with zero bars. Anything you log offline — snaps, reps, check-ins — queues locally and syncs the moment you're back on a connection. No data held hostage by stadium wifi.

Can I call defense with it too?

Yes. The D Caller is a separate tab beside the O Caller, built for the defensive loop: see the situation, see what their offense most likely runs, make your call, log the result — three taps or fewer per snap. It watches for tendency shifts live (what they're doing tonight vs. their season film) and flags them so you can adjust before they burn you twice. Coach-only; players never see it.

What does the in-game AI actually do?

It reads every snap you log and keeps a live analysis current under the call screen — drive summaries, tendency shifts, situational reads — with zero extra taps. You can also ask it questions in plain English — "what's beating us?", "what do they run on 3rd & long tonight?" — and get an answer built only from tonight's actual data, every number verified before it renders. Quarter and half are one-tap bookmarks that expand the same panel. The analysis engine is deterministic and runs on-device, so it works fully offline; typed questions and AI-written summaries layer in when you have a connection, and the preset questions answer instantly with zero bars. It never slows down the calling loop.

Who owns the playbook?

The program. Your plays live in your team's account with roles — Admin, Coach·Edit, Coach·View, Player — so the whole staff builds one system and every player studies the same library. Export your playbook as a file any time.

How do players join?

One join code. They sign up, tap "I'm a player", enter the code, pick their position, and start training. Coaches see every test score by player under Results.

What does the coach have to build before players can train?

Nothing. Reps Lab ships with built-in concepts versus six coverages, and the starter playbook is testable the moment you load it. Add your own plays and the tests grow with your system.
