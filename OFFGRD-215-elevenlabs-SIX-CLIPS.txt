----- BLOCK 2 -- CHART IT ONCE -----

Chart your film once.

OFF-GRID reads your breakdown the way you exported it. No re-tagging, no converting, no learning somebody else's names for your own formations. Map your vocabulary one time and it holds everywhere after.

Scout cards come out the other side, already built.


----- BLOCK 3 -- FRIDAY NIGHT - THE SITUATION -----

Friday night, you open O Caller.

It already knows the down, the distance, the hash, the field zone. Nothing to spin, nothing to set. Punt, downs, takeaway, score — it tracks the drive, the series, the quarter without you touching it.

The only thing on your screen is the call.


----- BLOCK 4 -- WHAT THEY'RE ABOUT TO GIVE YOU -----

Up top is what to expect. Their most likely front, their most likely coverage, how often they pressure — and the number of charted snaps behind all three, so you know how much to trust it.

Underneath, your own calls. Not a library. Your book, ranked for this exact situation, with what each one has done against this look.


----- BLOCK 5 -- ONE TAP -----

You make the call. One tap grades it.

That tap logs the play, updates your self-scout, sharpens the next prediction, and writes your call sheet while you stand there.

If the spotter got a down wrong, fix it in the log and everything downstream fixes itself.


----- BLOCK 6 -- IT TELLS YOU WHAT IT DOESN'T KNOW -----

And when it isn't sure, it says so.

Small sample gets flagged. A formation it can't resolve stays in your words and stays marked. It will not draw a rusher you never charted. Every count reconciles, and it shows you the receipt.

Nobody gets beat by the play they were told was a sixty percent tendency off four snaps.


----- BLOCK 7 -- THE OTHER SIDE, AND THE BOOTH -----

D Caller runs the same way against their offense. Their structure, their tendencies, your fronts and coverages, ranked the same way.

There's a booth mode for the box. A guided mode for a first-year coordinator, and one with the hand-holding turned off.

By Saturday morning your self-scout is written — call family, snaps, yards per play, efficiency. Nothing to type up.
