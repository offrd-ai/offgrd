# OFFGRD — what it doesn't do yet (told to buyers, on purpose)

Last updated 2026-09-15. This page is shown/said to every program before
they pay. Honesty sells; a surprised coach doesn't renew. Each item says
what the limit is, why, and what removes it.

## 1. Live yardage is bucketed; the film import replaces it

On game night the caller records gains in buckets (loss / short / solid /
chunk), because precise yardage isn't knowable from the sideline and we
won't pretend otherwise. When you import the charted film on Monday, the
exact `GN/LS` numbers replace the live taps automatically. Your season
book is chart-accurate; your in-game read is bucket-accurate. That's the
honest ceiling of a sideline tap.

## 2. Run lane and pass depth wake up when you tag GAP and PASS ZONE

"Run 62% · inside 58%" and "Pass → quick 60%" require the Hudl columns
GAP and PASS ZONE. No high school export we've seen ships with them
filled, so those readouts stay dormant until your staff tags them — two
extra taps per snap in Hudl. The code path is live today: the first
import with those columns filled lights up lane and depth automatically.
See the tagging guide for exactly what to type.

## 3. Formation-conditioned predictions need 8+ snaps per formation

"2x1 Wing: run 78%" only renders when we have at least 8 snaps of that
formation in the book. Below that we show the parent number instead of a
guess dressed up as a stat. Early in the season (or against a Week 1
opponent with one game of film) you'll see fewer formation lines — they
sharpen every week as imports accumulate. We show the deepest split the
data supports, never all of them stacked.

## 4. Hash-specific predictions need more snaps than a 2-game book has

Filtering tendencies by hash (left / middle / right) slices the data
three ways on top of down and distance. On a typical 2-game opponent book
those slices are single digits, and single digits aren't a read. The hash
chips work today; the honest answer at low sample sizes is the wider
number, and that's what we show. By mid-season books, hash splits carry
real weight.

## 5. Two devices on the SAME side of the same game: supported, not yet a proven workflow

O iPad + D iPad on one game is our standard, field-tested setup. Two
devices both logging *offense* (booth + sideline O) is supported by the
merge design — each device's snaps carry their own id and fold together —
but we have not yet run it in a real game, so we don't sell it as a
workflow. If you want to be the program that proves it, we'll support
you; we won't pretend it's routine.

---

## What we promise against these limits

- Your data is yours. After every game the log is written to your Files
  app automatically — you can hold your whole season in your hand.
- Nothing above loses a snap. Every limit is about how sharp a *read* we
  will claim, never about whether your game is recorded.
- Every limit on this page has a removal path, and the ones tied to
  tagging (2) are in your control this week.
